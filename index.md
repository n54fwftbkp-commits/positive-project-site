# Positive Project

The Positive Project examines overlooked positive effects, constructive interpretations, and the value that can emerge from subjects commonly treated as entirely negative.

The project does not claim that every consequence of a subject is good. It asks a narrower and often more useful question: **What good, reasonable, meaningful, or morally neutral qualities may also be present?**
