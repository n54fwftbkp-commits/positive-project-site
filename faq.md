# Frequently Asked Questions

## Does “not a bad thing” mean entirely good?

No. Most entries defend a limited proposition: the subject is not inherently, uniformly, or exclusively bad. Serious harms may still exist and should be stated directly.

## Is the project trying to provoke people?

The project uses strong titles to open a difficult question, but the intended method is constructive rather than antagonistic. The argument should stand on distinctions, benefits, context, and qualifications—not hostility toward people who disagree.

## Are entries permanent?

No. Entries may be corrected, clarified, expanded, or revised when stronger evidence or criticism is presented.

## Can I disagree publicly?

Yes. Counterarguments, complaints, factual concerns, and suggested improvements are welcome through the [Feedback page](../feedback/).

## Does the project endorse harmful conduct?

No. Finding a positive dimension in a broad subject does not excuse coercion, violence, deception, discrimination, preventable harm, or violations of other people’s rights.

## Who can access this website?

Anyone with the public link can read it. No ChatGPT or GitHub account is required to view the site. A GitHub account is required only to submit feedback through GitHub Issues.
