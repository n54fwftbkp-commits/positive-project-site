# Suggestions, Comments, Feedback, and Complaints

Thoughtful participation is welcome. Choose the option that best matches your purpose:

- [Suggest a new topic](https://github.com/n54fwftbkp-commits/positive-project-site/issues/new?template=topic-suggestion.yml)
- [Make a general comment](https://github.com/n54fwftbkp-commits/positive-project-site/issues/new?template=general-comment.yml)
- [Suggest an improvement](https://github.com/n54fwftbkp-commits/positive-project-site/issues/new?template=improvement.yml)
- [Report an error or factual concern](https://github.com/n54fwftbkp-commits/positive-project-site/issues/new?template=error-report.yml)
- [Present a counterargument or request clarification](https://github.com/n54fwftbkp-commits/positive-project-site/issues/new?template=counterargument.yml)
- [Make a complaint or report inappropriate content](https://github.com/n54fwftbkp-commits/positive-project-site/issues/new?template=complaint.yml)
- [Raise a privacy, copyright, or takedown concern](https://github.com/n54fwftbkp-commits/positive-project-site/issues/new?template=rights-concern.yml)
- [Report an accessibility or technical problem](https://github.com/n54fwftbkp-commits/positive-project-site/issues/new?template=technical-report.yml)

Submissions made through GitHub Issues are public. Do not include private medical, financial, contact, identity, security, or account information.

Every submission may be reviewed, but publication does not create an obligation to agree, respond individually, or make a requested change. Good-faith criticism is valued. Spam, threats, harassment, personal information, and unlawful content may be removed.
