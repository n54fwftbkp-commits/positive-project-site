# About the Positive Project

The Positive Project develops careful arguments for the proposition that a subject commonly described as bad may also contain constructive, beneficial, meaningful, or morally neutral dimensions.

The purpose is not denial, provocation for its own sake, or the erasure of harm. The purpose is to resist all-or-nothing thinking and make room for proportion, context, adaptation, human agency, and overlooked value.

Each entry attempts to identify a defensible positive case, state important limits, and distinguish the subject itself from particular harmful forms or consequences.

The project is independent and evolving. Published entries may be clarified or improved when strong criticism, better evidence, or clearer language becomes available.
